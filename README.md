🚴 Bike Sales & Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer demographic data and purchasing behavior for a bike retail business. Using **Microsoft Excel** (Pivot Tables, Advanced Formulas, and Data Visualization), an interactive dashboard was built to identify key customer segments, evaluate purchasing patterns, and uncover growth opportunities.

 📊 Dashboard Summary & Key Metrics

* Total Customers Analyzed: 1,000 customers
* Bike Purchasers: 481 customers
* Overall Purchase Rate (Conversion Rate): 48.1%
* Average Customer Income: $56,360
  
 🔍 Analytical Insights

1. Demographic Breakdown (Age & Gender)
* Middle-Aged Dominance: The Middle_Age segment (ages 31–54) represents the core revenue driver, accounting for 383 total bike purchases compared to 318 non-purchasers.
* Underperforming Segments: Adolescents (39 buyers vs. 71 non-buyers) and the Old age group (59 buyers vs. 130 non-buyers) show low purchase conversion and high drop-off rates.
* Gender Disparity: Male customers converted at a higher rate (269 buyers vs. 242 non-buyers) compared to Female customers (239 buyers vs. 250 non-buyers).

2. Purchasing Power & Economic Indicators
* Income Threshold: Customers who purchased a bike had a higher average income (**~$58,000**) compared to non-purchasers (**~$55,000**), indicating that higher disposable income directly correlates with purchase intent.

3. Commute Distance Dynamics
* Short-Commute High Intent: Customers living **0–1 Miles** and **2–5 Miles** from work represent the largest volume of buyers and highest conversion rates.
* Long-Distance Drop-Off: Customers commuting **10+ Miles** show a significant drop in conversion, likely due to traditional bicycles being impractical for long daily commutes.

 💡 Strategic Recommendations for Management

 🎯 Immediate / Short-Term Focus (30-Day Outlook)
1. **Optimize Ad Spend on High-Performing Demographics:** Direct marketing campaigns and social media targeting toward **Middle-Aged professionals** earning between **$55,000 and $60,000+**, as this group yields the highest ROI.
2. **Short-Commute Positioning:** Position bikes as an efficient, eco-friendly solution for short daily commutes (**0–5 miles**), highlighting convenience, parking savings, and fitness benefits.

 🚀 Long-Term Strategic Initiatives (Quarterly Roadmap)
1. Targeted Female Engagement Campaign: Address the lower conversion rate among female prospects. Conduct customer surveys or focus groups to evaluate potential barriers (e.g., product design, marketing representation, or pricing) and launch targeted promotions tailored to female riders.
2. Product Line Expansion (E-Bikes for Long Commuters): Introduce **Electric Bikes (E-Bikes)** or long-distance commuter accessories to unlock the under-converted 10+ Miles commute segment.

 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
* Microsoft Excel: Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Calculated Fields, Slicers, Dashboard Formatting & Visualization
